About

A courier business based in Elazığ, founded in 2025

Sedat Çağlar - Kurye Faliyetleri is a sole proprietorship run by Sedat Çağlar. Our core job is to pick up your shipments and deliver them safely to their recipients.

  1. Contact us

    Tell us the type of shipment and the pickup and delivery addresses by phone or email.

  2. Pickup

    We agree on a suitable time and pick up your shipment from the address you specify.

  3. Delivery

    Your shipment is delivered to the recipient and you are informed once delivery is complete.
