Our Services

Details of the courier services we offer for individual and business shipments.

Parcel & Document Delivery

Door-to-door delivery of official documents, contracts, files and small to medium-sized parcels.

  • Pickup from the address you specify
  • Hand delivery to the recipient
  • Notification when delivery is complete

Same-Day Delivery

For shipments that must arrive today, pickup and delivery are scheduled within the same day.

  • Pickup time agreed together
  • Delivered within the same day
  • Please contact us in advance to confirm availability

Platform Courier Services

Working as a courier for food, grocery and similar delivery platforms, picking up orders from restaurants or stores and delivering them to customers.

  • Timely order pickup
  • Careful handling of products
  • Friendly delivery to the customer

E-commerce Deliveries

Delivering orders to recipients for online businesses and individual sellers.

  • Single or bulk pickup from the seller
  • Delivery to the recipient address
  • Delivery status updates to the seller

Urgent In-City Delivery

Direct pickup and priority delivery without extra stops for shipments that cannot wait.

  • Direct pickup
  • Priority delivery with no transfers
  • Forgotten documents, keys, items and more

Pricing & availability

Pricing depends on the type of shipment, distance and delivery time. Please contact us by phone or email before sending to get information.
