Legal grounds

Your personal data is processed on the following legal grounds under Article 5/2 of KVKK:

  • Being directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c)

  • Being necessary for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right (Art. 5/2-e)
  • Being necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller, provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Art. 5/2-f)

Where none of these grounds applies, your data is processed only with your explicit consent.

Your rights under Article 11 of KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK, by applying to the data controller you have the right to:

  1. Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  2. Request information if your personal data has been processed,
  3. Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used in line with that purpose,
  4. Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred,
  5. Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
  6. Request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions in Article 7 of KVKK,
  7. Request that operations under (e) and (f) be notified to third parties to whom your data was transferred,
  8. Object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
  9. Claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing of your data.
