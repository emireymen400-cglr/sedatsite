About Us

Sedat Çağlar - Kurye Faliyetleri is a courier business founded in 2025 in Elazığ, Türkiye.

Who we are

Sedat Çağlar - Kurye Faliyetleri is a sole proprietorship run by Sedat Çağlar and registered with the Harput Tax Office. Our business is located at Sürsürü Mah. Karahanlı Sok. No:9 İç Kapı No:4 Merkez/Elazığ, Türkiye.

What we do

Our main field of activity is courier services: parcel and document delivery, same-day delivery, courier work through delivery platforms, delivery of e-commerce orders and urgent in-city shipments. Our service area is Türkiye; please contact us before sending to confirm coverage and availability.

Our mobile app work

Alongside our courier work, we develop mobile apps for the Android platform. Each published app is listed on the Our Apps page with its own detail page and privacy policy. Our apps are published on Google Play with Sedat Çağlar - Kurye Faliyetleri as the developer.

Our principles

  • Working transparently with accurate and verifiable company details
  • Handling shipments carefully and keeping customers informed throughout
  • Processing personal data only as needed for the service and in line with the law
